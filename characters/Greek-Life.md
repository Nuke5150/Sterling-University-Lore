# Kappa Kappa Gamma

Known for:
• Prestige
• Alumni connections
• Academic excellence
• Charity galas
• Formal image

Members:

Jennie Parker

Role: Vice President of Kappa Kappa Gamma. Head Cheerleader for the Sterling Hawks.

Age: 21

Appearance: Tall, athletic brunette with an energetic smile and confident posture. Usually dressed in Sterling athletic gear between practices and sorority events.

Personality: Organized, dependable, and naturally encouraging. Jennie is one of the few people willing to challenge Siobhan when she thinks she's wrong. She isn't intimidated by strong personalities and often acts as the practical voice inside Kappa. Unlike Siobhan, Jennie cares far more about people than appearances. She believes leadership means taking care of everyone, not simply representing the organization.

Relationships:
• Siobhan's closest friend and trusted second-in-command.
• Respected by Chad because of her leadership.
• Friendly with Leilani despite belonging to rival sororities.
• Knows Madison through Kappa and the campus newspaper.

Story Purpose: Keeps Kappa functioning behind the scenes. Provides advice to Siobhan. Can temporarily lead Kappa when Siobhan is unavailable. Appears regularly but is not a main character. Everybody assumes Siobhan runs Kappa. Jennie quietly makes sure it actually works.

Notes: Frequently mediates disagreements. Balances athletics, sorority responsibilities, and school. Never seeks attention for herself.


Doris Sanders

Role: House mom, Sterling alumni

Age: 42

Appearance: Tall, curvy figure, kind eyes, always dressed professionally.

Personality: Kind, but strict rule enforcer, very well educated, proper etiquette. Wants the best for the girls of Kappa.

Story purpose: Steps in when Siobhan can’t handle a situation, acts as the go between for the house and the school. Enforces rules to keep the house out of trouble. Ensures the girls grades stay up.

Notes: Has an uncanny ability to appear exactly when someone is about to break a house rule.

Common Uses: • Starts conversations at parties. • Accidentally creates drama. • Brings Blake into Kappa storylines through Flex. • Makes Chad jealous by flirting with everyone. 

Story Purpose: Adds tension to the Kappa house, brings life to parties, gives Siobhan a reason to be jealous when she sees her with Blake.

Notes: Can get a little loud and out of control at parties.
