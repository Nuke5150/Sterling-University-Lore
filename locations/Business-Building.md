# Business Building

## Overview

The Business Building is one of Sterling University's busiest academic buildings.

Home to the College of Business, it houses lecture halls, faculty offices, study spaces, project rooms, and computer labs. Students often spend entire afternoons here moving between classes, team meetings, and office hours.

Unlike the quiet atmosphere of the Library, the Business Building is active throughout the day with students discussing assignments, preparing presentations, and meeting project groups.

---

# Atmosphere

The building feels modern, practical, and constantly in use.

Glass walls allow natural light into the main lobby.

Digital displays announce upcoming lectures, club meetings, internship fairs, and recruiting events.

Students frequently gather in the lobby between classes.

---

# Layout

### First Floor

- Main Lobby
- Lecture Halls
- Student Lounge
- Vending Area
- Career Services Office

The lobby remains busy between classes.

Students often stop here before heading to class or waiting for friends.

---

### Second Floor

- Classrooms
- Faculty Offices
- Advising Center

Students regularly visit professors during office hours.

Hallways are quieter than the lobby.

---

### Third Floor

- Computer Labs
- Group Project Rooms
- Study Areas

Most group assignments are completed here.

Project rooms are commonly reserved weeks in advance.

---

# Student Lounge

The lounge serves as an informal meeting place.

Contains:

- Comfortable chairs
- Small tables
- Whiteboards
- Charging stations
- Vending machines

Students often review notes, wait for class, or work on group projects.

---

# Group Project Rooms

Small glass-walled rooms designed for team assignments.

Each room includes:

- Large monitor
- Whiteboard
- Conference table

Students frequently reserve these rooms for several hours.

Many friendships begin here.

So do disagreements.

---

# Computer Lab

Available to all business students.

Commonly used for:

- Spreadsheets
- Data analysis
- Financial modeling
- Presentations

The lab remains busy throughout the semester.

---

# Faculty Offices

Professors maintain regular office hours.

Students often wait in the hallway before appointments.

Informal conversations frequently happen outside office doors.

---

# Career Services

Hosts:

- Internship information
- Resume reviews
- Employer recruiting
- Networking workshops

Upperclassmen visit frequently.

---

# Daily Rhythm

Morning

Students arrive carrying coffee.

Lecture halls gradually fill.

---

Late Morning

Hallways become crowded between classes.

Students gather outside classrooms waiting for professors.

---

Afternoon

Project meetings begin.

Study rooms remain occupied.

The Student Lounge becomes busiest.

---

Evening

Many students stay to finish group assignments.

The building slowly quiets after sunset.

---

# Typical Background Activity

Without involving the main characters, the building often contains:

- Students rehearsing presentations.
- Someone searching for an empty study room.
- Clubs promoting networking events.
- Recruiters setting up information tables.
- Faculty chatting in hallways.
- Laptops open on nearly every table.
- Whiteboards covered with diagrams.
- Someone rushing because they're late for class.

---

# Story Purpose

The Business Building naturally creates repeated encounters.

Students often recognize familiar faces from previous classes without knowing their names.

Running into the same person several times during a semester feels normal.

Many relationships begin through repeated academic contact rather than planned meetings.

For Blake, the building gradually becomes familiar as he settles into Sterling.

For Siobhan, it is already part of her daily routine.
