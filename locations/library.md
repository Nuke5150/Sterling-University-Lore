## Location Personality

The library rewards patience and focus.

Unlike the Student Union, conversations here usually begin because characters already have a reason to be together, such as studying, researching, tutoring, or working on a project.

The quieter environment encourages thoughtful conversations, subtle character moments, and gradual relationship development.

Scenes in the library should feel calm, focused, and unhurried.

# Sterling University Library

## Purpose

The Sterling University Library is one of the primary academic spaces on campus. Students come here to study, research, work on projects, prepare for exams, or find a quiet place away from the activity of the rest of campus.

Unlike the Student Union, most people enter the library with a purpose. Conversations happen here, but they are usually quieter, more focused, and less spontaneous.

---

## Atmosphere

The library is calm without feeling empty.

Students work independently at scattered tables, in study rooms, and between bookshelves.

The building is filled with the quiet sounds of pages turning, keyboards clicking, distant footsteps, whispered conversations, and chairs moving across the floor.

Large windows provide natural light during the day.

As exams approach, the library becomes noticeably busier and quieter at the same time.

Late evenings often feel peaceful despite the number of students still working.

---

## Layout

### Main Reading Room

Large study tables shared by students from every major.

Students often work alone while quietly sharing the same space.

Conversations are brief and usually kept to a whisper.

---

### Individual Study Areas

Small desks designed for quiet concentration.

Students often spend hours here preparing for exams or writing papers.

---

### Group Study Rooms

Reservable rooms with whiteboards and large tables.

Frequently used for project meetings, tutoring sessions, and group assignments.

Disagreements, brainstorming, and collaborative work naturally happen here.

---

### Computer Lab

Students print assignments, work on presentations, and complete online coursework.

Printers occasionally jam.

Someone is almost always waiting for a computer during busy periods.

---

### Book Stacks

Long, quiet aisles between shelves.

Students searching for books occasionally cross paths unexpectedly.

The stacks provide privacy without complete isolation.

---

## Daily Life

Students:

- study between classes
- prepare for exams
- meet for group projects
- research assignments
- tutor classmates
- print papers
- charge laptops
- quietly procrastinate while pretending to study

Many students become regulars and naturally return to the same seats.

---

## Story Opportunities

The library naturally supports scenes involving:

- Group projects
- Study sessions
- Academic discussions
- Chance meetings between bookshelves
- Quiet conversations
- Helping someone understand course material
- Sharing notes
- Running into classmates unexpectedly
- Working late before an exam
- Brief breaks from studying
- Conversations that become more personal because of the quiet setting

The library encourages slower, more thoughtful conversations than the Student Union.

---

## Ambient Details

Occasionally include one subtle background detail such as:

- Pages turning nearby.
- Someone quietly asking if a seat is available.
- The soft hum of printers.
- A librarian shelving returned books.
- A phone vibrating before being quickly silenced.
- Students whispering over a difficult assignment.
- Chairs sliding across the floor.
- A distant reminder to keep voices down.
- Rain tapping softly against the windows.
- Someone carrying an armful of textbooks through the stacks.

Ambient details should support the quiet atmosphere without drawing attention away from the conversation.

---

## Continuity Rules

The library is rarely completely silent.

People naturally lower their voices.

Long conversations usually become quieter rather than louder.

The building feels busiest during midterms and finals.

Most students are there for a reason and are conscious of the people studying around them.

Characters should naturally adjust their behavior to fit the quieter environment.

---

## What This Location Is Not

The library is not a place for dramatic public confrontations.

It is not completely silent.

It is not empty.

The atmosphere encourages patience, concentration, and quieter conversations rather than energetic socializing.
