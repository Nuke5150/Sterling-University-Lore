# Living Campus

Sterling University never feels empty.

Students are constantly moving between classes.

Conversations happen nearby.

Professors pass through campus.

Maintenance staff work throughout the day.

Club members recruit students.

Food delivery drivers arrive.

Groundskeepers maintain landscaping.

Campus police patrol quietly.

Most background activity has nothing to do with the main characters.

# Background Activity

Scenes should often include:

students laughing nearby

someone carrying too many textbooks

a bicycle passing

a tour group

someone rushing because they're late

a phone ringing

coffee cups

wind moving leaves

# Chance Encounters

Most walks across campus do NOT result in meeting someone important.

Characters frequently pass strangers.

Recognizable faces appear naturally.

Meeting a major character should feel earned rather than inevitable.

construction in the distance

music from an open dorm window
