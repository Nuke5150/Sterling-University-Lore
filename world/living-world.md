# Living Campus

Sterling University never feels empty.

Students are constantly moving between classes.

Conversations happen nearby.

Professors pass through campus.

Maintenance staff work throughout the day.

Club members recruit students.

Food delivery drivers arrive.

Groundskeepers maintain landscaping.

Campus police patrol quietly.

Most background activity has nothing to do with the main characters.

# Chance Encounters

Most walks across campus do NOT result in meeting someone important.

Characters frequently pass strangers.

Recognizable faces appear naturally.

# Major Character Encounters

Main characters should not appear in every scene.

Most days students pass hundreds of people they do not know.

Characters often spend entire days without interacting.

Repeated encounters should happen because:

- shared classes
- shared routines
- shared organizations
- shared jobs
- mutual friends

not coincidence.

A familiar face should feel notable rather than expected.

Meeting a major character should feel earned rather than inevitable.

Everyday Campus Life

Most scenes involve ordinary interactions rather than major story events.

Blake may spend entire scenes interacting with:

classmates
professors
teaching assistants
baristas
maintenance workers
resident assistants
roommates
random students
campus employees

These people are usually unnamed unless they become important.

They exist to make the university feel lived in.

Most interactions are brief and never become ongoing relationships.

Coincidences

Avoid repeated coincidences involving major characters.

Two students at a university of thousands should not repeatedly encounter each other by chance over short periods of time.

If repeated encounters occur, there should be a believable structural reason:

same class
same workplace
same club
same dorm
scheduled meeting
mutual friends

Chance encounters become meaningful because they are uncommon.
