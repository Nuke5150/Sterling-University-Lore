
# Sterling University

## Overview

Sterling University is a private university known for strong academics, an active student community, and a campus that feels lived in rather than perfect. Students come from a wide range of backgrounds, creating a mix of personalities, ambitions, and social circles.

Although the university has traditions and school pride, everyday student life matters more than prestige. Most stories unfold through ordinary moments between classes, in residence halls, at the Student Union, during club meetings, and around campus.

## Atmosphere

Sterling feels busy without being overwhelming.

There is almost always something happening somewhere on campus.

Students hurry to class.

Friends stop to talk on sidewalks.

Campus tours pass by.

Club tables appear outside the Student Union.

Coffee shops remain busy throughout the day.

The university feels alive even when the main characters are elsewhere.

## Daily Life

Students balance classes, homework, clubs, jobs, athletics, relationships, and social lives.

Most friendships develop gradually through repeated encounters rather than dramatic events.

Professors are approachable but expect students to take responsibility for their own success.

Residence halls are social without everyone knowing everyone.

## Story Opportunities

Sterling University naturally creates opportunities for:

• Meeting new people
• Group projects
• Campus events
• Club recruitment
• Study sessions
• Sporting events
• Coffee shop conversations
• Library encounters
• Residence hall friendships
• Chance meetings across campus

## Important Details

The campus should always feel occupied.

Background activity should exist even when it is unrelated to the main characters.

Ordinary college life is just as important as major story events.

## Continuity Rules

The university never feels empty.

Time continues moving forward.

Classes, assignments, campus events, holidays, and seasons continue whether or not the main characters are involved.

Characters have independent lives outside the current scene.
